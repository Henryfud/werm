# 07. Reading list and citation status

Every journal, conference, book and preprint citation in this project was looked up on **2026-10-05** in Crossref, Europe PMC, arXiv or the publisher's own page. Each row gives the citation as the record has it, its DOI, and a status:

- **verified**: title, first author, year, venue, volume and pages match the record.
- **corrected**: the earlier entry in this file was wrong. The citation below is the record's version, and the note says what changed.
- **could not verify**: no primary record could be fetched. Do not use it in public copy.

Author lists give every author for four or fewer, otherwise the first author and "et al.". Initials of co-authors were not checked one by one.

Only verified or corrected entries may appear on the site, in the README or in any public post.
