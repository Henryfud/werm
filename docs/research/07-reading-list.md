## How to close out the "recall" entries

For each, search the title, open the publisher or PubMed page, and fix the year, volume, pages and author list. Change the status in this file to **verified** with a date. Do not leave unverified citations in the site notes, the README or any social post. The site's Sources list currently includes only items marked **seen**. Cook et al. 2019 appears there with its citation written from memory (the dataset listing was seen, the volume and pages were not), so verify that one first.
